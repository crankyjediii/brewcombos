    Ordering at 7 Brew

    How do I order a secret menu drink at 7 Brew?
    Say it in this order: size, hot or iced (or Chiller), the base drink, then your flavors and any extras. Every order this site writes follows that pattern. If your Brewista doesn't know a drink by its nickname, just read them the flavors.
    What is a Chiller at 7 Brew?
    A Chiller is 7 Brew's blended, slushy version of a drink. Coffee drinks, 7 Energy, lemonade and more can be made as a Chiller.
    What is a soft top?
    Soft top is 7 Brew's creamy topping for cold drinks, similar to cold foam. Add it to iced drinks and Chillers.
    Can I get any drink sugar-free?
    Most flavors come in a sugar-free version, and 7 Energy has a sugar-free base. Tick sugar-free here and the order line says it for you. Sugar-free options vary by stand, so ask if yours is missing.
    How many flavors can I add?
    Three flavors is the usual limit. You can also ask for ¼, ½ or extra sweetness to change how strong the syrups taste.
